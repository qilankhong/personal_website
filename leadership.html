Leadership & volunteering

Terrapalooza

Programming Committee Member & Presenter · Polytechnic School · 2025–present

Terrapalooza is Poly's Earth Day festival. On the programming committee I design climate-education workshops and speaker events. At the 2026 festival I presented a climate constitution to peers and faculty that draws on behavioral economics, intergenerational equity and environmental design policy, and shared the story behind Vermighty.

Poly Global Voices Club

Head of Operations · 2025–present

Global Voices is a student-led club connecting high schoolers worldwide to discuss global issues. I run communications and coordinate with partner schools in Australia, Singapore and Finland. I've helped host a school event with visiting professor Dr. Steven Lamy, presented research on the Fukushima wastewater release and co-organized a school-wide clothing swap.

GNEC Hackathon

Partnerships Coordinator & Judge · Global NGO Executive Committee · 2024–present

After placing second in the 2024 hackathon, I stayed on as an intern. The role began with social-media graphics and grew into leading partner and sponsor outreach, designing marketing and branding, moderating opening and closing ceremonies and judging. I've judged over 600 finalist presentations, and I built an automated certificate-distribution system to replace a manual process.

The Huntington

Museum Education & Gardening Volunteer · 2025–present

I lead hands-on cultural learning for visitors in the Chinese Garden's Flowery Brush Library and help run sustainability-focused events such as the Huntington's plant sale.

Boys & Girls Club of Pasadena

Volunteer · 2025–present

I volunteer weekly with after-school enrichment and leisure activities, and I've brought Leaf by Leaf workshops to several Boys & Girls Club locations.

Global Scholar

Polytechnic School · 2026–present

As a Global Scholar I attend speaker events with diplomats and policy experts, take part in cross-cultural exchanges and write a blog analyzing international affairs, from geopolitical conflicts to global environmental policy. I also serve as a Global Ambassador, helping organize events and discussions that build global awareness on campus.
