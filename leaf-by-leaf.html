Leaf by Leaf

I co-founded Leaf by Leaf after noticing that children are told which foods are healthy without much say in the choice. Our workshops have kids plant and care for food themselves, then decide whether to eat what they grew.

Visit the Leaf by Leaf website

Workshops

20+ workshops · 600+ people reached

I've designed and led workshops at schools, youth centers, a rescue mission, a children's museum and community events, in person and virtually.

Our children's book and growing kits

We wrote and published an original children's book and created take-home growing kits that extend the workshops. We've donated many of both.
